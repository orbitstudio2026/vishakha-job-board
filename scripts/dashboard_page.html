How this board works

Sources

Employer career sites (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday feeds), Canada's Job Bank, and Indeed through the Indeed connector and Job Bank. Each row says where its salary came from; board salaries are what the employer advertised there and are not cross-checked on the employer's site.

Salary

Third-party averages (Glassdoor, Payscale) appear only in a row's details and are never shown as its salary. Hourly pay is converted at the posted weekly hours × 52, or 37.5 hours when none are stated. No salary is shown as "Not disclosed".

Violet tags

Top match means a score of — or more. Pays above median means at least — above its category median. No commission, Hybrid or remote, Health & dental, Bonus and Right level come from the posting text.

Screen and score

Commission roles are scored for sales intensity; High (60+) is screened out but stays under "Screened out". The match score weighs category fit, resume skills, location and level. Salary does not change the score.
